Why finance

My family's money problem was never income. It was knowledge. Nobody taught us how money works, and not knowing is expensive in a quiet way that takes years to show up rather than arriving all at once.

So I got curious about how wealthy people actually use capital to make more of it, and I decided I would rather learn it properly than keep guessing. That is the whole reason I am in finance. It is also why I tend to hand people my notes instead of keeping them.

The Greenwood Project

Greenwood trains minority students for finance. Behavioral prep, technicals, and the part nobody inherits, which is the network. I found the recruiter and talked to him before I ever applied, because I wanted him to meet a person rather than read a file. Two rounds later I was in.

What I got certified in

Accounting, valuation, M&A, reading filings, Excel, PowerPoint, certified through Financeable.

That is the list. The rest of this site is where the list stopped being enough.

Project Destined

Project Destined is where the real estate work came from. This is the model: a 31 unit deal at 17 million, ten year hold, underwritten start to finish. NOI, cap rates, rent rolls clicked for me immediately, and this is the artifact of that.

What the model says

Going in at a 5.83% cap against 7.00% debt, so leverage is dilutive at entry. That is why the levered return of 10.54% barely clears the unlevered 9.45%, even at roughly half loan to value. Debt yield is 12.0% and coverage is 1.50 times, so the deal is safe. The loan just is not helping early.

Three years of renovation at 1.715 million flow through the cash flow, which is why the first three years run negative before debt. Exit is a 5.00% cap on year eleven NOI of 1.74 million.

The DCF

I could build one before I understood a single line of it. NOI, cap rates, rent rolls: those clicked immediately. The DCF I was only operating, and I knew the difference even when nobody was checking.

What changed

A company is worth the cash it will hand you, less the fact that cash later is worth less than cash now. Every row in the model is that one sentence, itemized.

I got there by finding upperclassmen who already knew it and asking them to explain it until the reason showed up instead of the steps. That meant being willing to sound behind in a room where I was supposed to be ahead. I have found that trade is almost always worth making.

Being the advanced one

At Greenwood I have been ahead of my peers on technicals. I do not enjoy it as much as I expected to.

Why not

Mostly it means I asked for help earlier than they did. Being called advanced sets a ceiling I have not earned, and it quietly changes how people talk to me. They stop telling me where my holes are, which is the only information I actually want.

It also puts distance between me and the people I would rather be learning beside. So I try to be the first one in the room to say I do not know something, because that usually gives everyone else permission to say it too. Still patching.

Casual and professional at once

Networking taught me the tactical parts. When to reach out again, how to find someone's contact information. Register is the one I still have not solved, and it matters more than the tactics, because it decides whether someone remembers you or remembers a template.

LooseStiff

Tesla

The $7,500 federal credit died in September 2025 and took the US EV market with it. New EV sales fell 28% year over year in Q1 2026. Tesla grew through it, and that gap is the whole thesis.

Hover or focus a bar for the underlying figures.

The argument

Tesla delivered 358,023 vehicles in Q1 2026, up about 6% against the same quarter a year earlier, then 480,126 in Q2, up 25%. That is its first annual growth after two straight years of decline.

A demand shock that severe is the cheapest way a leader takes share. It prices out the buyers who were only ever there for the subsidy, and it strands the manufacturers who were only ever competitive with it. I want to own the company that gets bigger while the market gets smaller.

Where it breaks

Tesla posted its best Q2 ever and the stock fell 7% that day. If a 25% beat moves the price down, then deliveries are not what is setting it, and my mechanism of volumes recovering and the stock following does not hold.

BYD also outsold Tesla 2.26 million to 1.64 million in 2025, so the survivor story is a US story and not a global one. I think I am right about the share shift. I am less sure that is the same thing as being right about the stock, and I would rather say so than find out in an interview that I had not noticed.

Written September 2026, on the Q1 and Q2 2026 delivery reports. Q3 lands in early October and could break this.

The notes I gave away

The Howard University Investment Group, HUIG, is the student investment fund at Howard. Stock pitches, mentorship, and the fundamentals taught properly. Two interview rounds, somewhere between 100 and 115 applicants, and 10 to 15 seats.

Last year I gave someone my notes for the HUIG application while I was applying to it myself. He got in. I did not. I sat with that for a while, and where I landed is that I would rather be the person people come to than the person who wins by staying quiet. I am applying again this year, and I helped someone else again.

From the bottom, the top of a mountain looks like a point. Climb one and the top is flat. There is more room up there than it looks like from down here.
